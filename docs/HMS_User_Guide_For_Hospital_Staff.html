SR-AIIMS HMS

Hospital Management System User Guide

A simple operating manual for reception, doctors, nursing, laboratory, pharmacy, Panchkarma, accounts, HR, housekeeping, inventory, and administration teams.

Audience: Non-technical hospital staff and department heads

Purpose: Explain the daily flow, screen usage, role responsibilities, and safe working practices

Prepared for: Shanti-Ratnam / SR-AIIMS hospital operations

Version: Staff training edition - 2026-06-01

1. Whole HMS Working Flow

The HMS connects patient registration, appointment booking, consultation, tests, medicine, therapy, admission, billing, and reporting. Each department updates its own part of the work so the next department can continue without repeating data entry.

1. LoginStaff logs in with their own username and password. The menu changes according to role.
2. Patient RegistryReception searches existing patient or registers a new patient. UHID / registration number is generated by system.
3. AppointmentReception books doctor, date, time slot, department, and complaint. Token/queue appears for today.
Login -> Patient -> Appointment -> OPD Queue
4. Screening / VitalsNursing or reception records BP, pulse, temperature, weight, height, SPO2, and RR.
5. Doctor OPDDoctor opens queue, records assessment, diagnosis, prescription, lab order, therapy advice, and discharge summary.
6. Lab / Pharmacy / TherapyLab collects samples and enters results. Pharmacy dispenses medicines. Panchkarma schedules and completes therapies.
Screening -> Doctor -> Lab / Pharmacy / Panchkarma
7. BillingAccounts, reception, or allowed staff create bills, collect payments, and print invoices.
8. IPD / RoomsIf admitted, patient is assigned room/bed, bedside notes and vitals are recorded, therapies are scheduled, then discharge is completed.
9. Reports and AdminManagement reviews OPD, IPD, revenue, pharmacy, lab, Panchkarma, inventory, and HR reports.

Important habit: Always search before creating a new patient. This avoids duplicate UHID and keeps the patient's history in one place.

2. Basic Screen Use for Everyone

  1. Open the HMS web link given by admin.
  2. Login with your assigned username and password.
  3. Use the left side menu to open your department screen.
  4. Use search boxes to find patient, bill, appointment, item, or order.
  5. Fill required details carefully. Do not guess medical, billing, or identity information.
  6. Click the main button such as Register Patient, Book Appointment, Save Results, or Collect Payment.
  7. Read success or error message shown on screen. If an error appears, correct the details and try again.
  8. Logout or lock the computer when leaving the desk.

3. Role-Wise Training Guide

Reception / Front Desk

Main responsibility: Patient entry, appointment booking, queue control, basic OPD/IPD coordination, and some billing support.

Daily flow

  1. Open Patients and search by UHID, registration number, OPD/IPD number, phone, ID number, father name, or patient name.
  2. If patient is new, click Add Patient and enter patient type, name, father name, DOB, gender, phone, address, ID details, emergency contact, and referred by.
  3. Open Appointments, select existing patient, doctor, department, date, time slot, type, source, and problem.
  4. Watch Reception queue board for today's patients.
  5. In OPD, start or load consultation when patient reaches OPD desk.
  6. If allowed at your desk, generate OPD bill or guide patient to accounts.
  7. For admitted patient, coordinate IPD admission and room/bed assignment.

Do and do not

  • Do search first before adding a patient.
  • Do enter correct mobile number because later search and communication depend on it.
  • Do not create duplicate patient records for follow-up visits.
  • Do not cancel appointments unless requested by patient, doctor, or authorized staff.

Screening / Nursing Desk

Main responsibility: Record patient vitals before or during OPD/IPD care.

OPD screening flow

  1. Open OPD and choose the patient from the OPD queue.
  2. Click start/load consultation if required.
  3. Enter vitals: BP, pulse, temperature, weight, height, SPO2, and respiratory rate.
  4. Click Save Vitals. The visit is forwarded for doctor consultation.

IPD nursing flow

  1. Open IPD and select active admission.
  2. Add progress notes when required.
  3. Record bedside vitals: BP, pulse, temp, SPO2, RR, weight, and notes.
  4. Check scheduled therapies and bed/room status.

Doctors

Main responsibility: Clinical assessment, diagnosis, prescription, lab order, therapy advice, follow-up, and visit completion.

OPD consultation flow

  1. Open OPD and filter by doctor if needed.
  2. Select patient from queue and load the visit.
  3. Review patient details, complaint, and vitals.
  4. Enter Ayurvedic assessment such as prakriti, nadi pariksha, agni, koshtha, vikriti, and observations.
  5. Enter diagnosis, complaints, medicines, dose, frequency, route, duration, anupana, and instructions.
  6. Add diet, lifestyle, yoga, Panchkarma, specialized therapy, investigation advice, and follow-up date.
  7. Create lab order if tests are required.
  8. Save discharge summary or print patient copy when needed.
  9. Generate OPD bill if allowed, or ask accounts/reception to bill.
  10. Click Complete Visit only after consultation is final.

Good practice

  • Save prescription before sending patient to pharmacy.
  • Create lab order before asking patient to go to laboratory.
  • Use clear medicine instructions so pharmacy and patient can understand.

Laboratory

Main responsibility: Receive lab orders, collect samples, enter results, print reports, and create lab invoice if needed.

  1. Open Laboratory.
  2. Select the lab order from Lab Register / Order board.
  3. Check patient information, ordered tests, doctor, and priority.
  4. Enter sample type, collection time, and collection note.
  5. Click Collect Sample.
  6. Enter processing summary and test results, including value, flag, and remarks.
  7. Click Save Results.
  8. Use Print Report to give the patient or doctor a printed copy.
  9. Create lab invoice from the billing link if the lab order is not already billed.

Pharmacy

Main responsibility: Dispense prescribed medicines, maintain stock, receive batches, and review stock alerts.

  1. Open Pharmacy.
  2. Select prescription from Prescription Queue.
  3. Review patient name, doctor, medicines, dose, duration, and instructions.
  4. Check stock alerts before dispensing.
  5. Dispense medicine lines and complete the prescription.
  6. For new stock, use Receive Stock and enter medicine, supplier, batch number, expiry date, quantity, purchase price, selling price, and note.
  7. Review Recent Dispensing for completion history.
Pharmacy caution: Always verify medicine name, batch, expiry, and quantity before handing medicine to patient.

Panchkarma / Therapist

Main responsibility: Schedule, start, complete, and bill therapy sessions with material usage tracking.

  1. Open Panchkarma.
  2. For new session, select patient, therapy, therapist, recommended by, room, date, time, duration, recovery bed if needed, complaint, and preparation notes.
  3. Click Schedule Session.
  4. Use Daily Board to see the day's therapy queue.
  5. Select session and click start when patient arrives.
  6. Enter execution notes, outcome, follow-up advice, session charge, payment status, and materials used.
  7. Click Complete Session.

Accounts / Billing

Main responsibility: Create invoices, collect payments, print receipts, and monitor balance amounts.

  1. Open Billing.
  2. To create manual invoice, choose patient and bill type.
  3. Add discount, tax, notes, doctor details, patient address override if needed, and bill items.
  4. For each item, enter description/product name, category, quantity, unit price, and pharmacy batch/expiry details if pharmacy bill.
  5. Click Create Bill.
  6. Select bill from Bill register.
  7. For payment, enter amount, payment mode, reference number, and note.
  8. Click Collect Payment.
  9. Click Print Invoice when patient needs copy.
Billing caution: Discounts, refunds, and corrections must follow hospital approval policy. Add clear reason notes.

IPD / Ward Team

Main responsibility: Manage admission, bed, daily notes, vitals, therapy, discharge, and IPD billing.

  1. Open IPD and use Admit Patient.
  2. Select patient, room, bed, attending doctor, admission source, date, time, expected discharge, reason, diagnosis, and initial note.
  3. Click Admit Patient.
  4. Select the admission from IPD register.
  5. Add clinical notes and bedside vitals regularly.
  6. Schedule IPD therapy if prescribed.
  7. At discharge, enter date, time, status, condition, stay days override if needed, bed status after discharge, extra charge, discharge summary, advice, and bed note.
  8. Tick Create IPD bill on discharge when billing should be generated.
  9. Click Discharge Patient.

Rooms / Housekeeping

Main responsibility: Keep bed status clear so reception and IPD staff know what is available.

  1. Open Rooms.
  2. Check room register for available, occupied, cleaning, and maintenance beds.
  3. Select a room, then select bed.
  4. For available bed, assign patient and enter admission type, expected discharge, and note.
  5. For occupied bed, discharge bed and set next status: cleaning, available, or maintenance.
  6. Use cleaning status until room is ready for next patient.

Inventory / Store

Main responsibility: Track non-pharmacy hospital items such as linen, housekeeping material, equipment, and consumables.

  1. Open Inventory.
  2. Create item with name, category, department, unit, opening quantity, reorder level, location, supplier, purchase price, and notes.
  3. Use Stock Movement for receipt, issue, or adjustment.
  4. Enter item, type, quantity, department, reference number, and note.
  5. Click Update Stock.
  6. Review hospital store and movement history regularly.

HR

Main responsibility: Maintain staff records, attendance, leave, duty roster, payroll, and documents.

  1. Open HR.
  2. Create or update employee profile with joining date, employment type/status, emergency contact, salary, and notes.
  3. Review daily attendance sheet and department attendance.
  4. Add leave entry with employee, leave type, start date, end date, and reason.
  5. Create shifts with name, start, end, break minutes, and grace minutes.
  6. Assign employee to shift with effective date and week off.
  7. Add payroll with employee, month, basic salary, allowances, deductions, and status.
  8. Add employee document metadata such as type, name, number, issue date, expiry date, and file URL.

Admin / Management

Main responsibility: Supervise system usage, users, roles, reports, approvals, and operational quality.

  1. Use Users to review imported staff directory and department/role mix.
  2. Use Reports for daily OPD, IPD census, revenue, pharmacy sales, lab workload, and Panchkarma performance.
  3. Check duplicate patient problems, unpaid bills, stock alerts, pending lab results, and incomplete visits.
  4. Approve high-value discounts, refunds, billing corrections, and stock adjustments as per hospital policy.
  5. Make sure staff use their own login. Do not allow shared passwords.

7. Data Quality and Safety Rules

  • Use your own login only. Never share passwords.
  • Search before creating a patient.
  • Use correct spelling for patient name and father name.
  • Enter phone numbers carefully; they are used for searching and communication.
  • Do not leave required fields blank if the information is available.
  • Do not use another department's role to bypass permission.
  • For bills, refunds, discounts, and corrections, add notes and follow approval process.
  • For pharmacy, verify batch, expiry, and quantity.
  • For lab, verify patient and order before collecting sample.
  • For discharge, confirm all clinical, billing, bed, and medicine steps are complete.

8. Common Problems and What to Do

ProblemWhat staff should do
Patient not foundSearch by phone, father name, UHID, registration number, and alternate spelling. If still not found, ask reception/admin before creating new record.
Wrong patient selectedStop before saving. Go back and select correct patient. Inform admin if wrong information was already saved.
Button is disabledYour role may not have permission, or required information is missing. Ask the responsible department or admin.
Appointment slot not availableSelect another available slot or another date. Emergency override is not generally allowed.
Bill already existsOpen Billing and review existing invoice instead of creating a duplicate.
Lab result entered wronglyInform lab lead/admin immediately for correction process.
Medicine stock low or expiredDo not dispense from expired batch. Update stock and inform pharmacy lead/admin.
Bed still showing occupied after dischargeOpen Rooms/IPD and set bed status to cleaning, available, or maintenance as applicable.
Training tip: During first week, each department should practice with sample patients under supervision before using the HMS independently on live patients.
